200 smuggling runs logged: exact bribe costs, real risk numbers, and why I stopped travelling light

Ignivar's guide is what got me started and it's still what I go by. I've been logging every run since then, around 200 trades and 69 customs stops, mostly to work out my own routes. Some of it turned out to be worth sharing.

Most of it holds up exactly. Price band of 0.85x to 1.43x is spot on, buy prices all match, market shifts every 10 minutes like it says.

Customs risk reads lower for me than the formula. Straight off the gauge: 0% full = 5%, 64% = 34%, 83% = 42%, 100% = 50%. Works out to roughly 5 + (fullness x 0.45). I've never seen it above 50%, even on a full hold. If anyone's seen higher, say so.

Bribes are exactly 25% of cargo value. Every time. 69 stops, no variation at all. So 31 Diamonds is $155,000 of cargo and always a $38,750 bribe. You can work your exact cost out before you leave.

That 25% is behind the thing that surprised me most. Filling your hold beats playing it safe, even though you get stopped more often. The bribe is a percentage, not a flat fee, so every extra unit brings its own along with it. Same 25% whether you carry 5 or 31. So an extra unit still pays as long as its margin beats 25% of the buy price, which most runs clear easily. Going from 20 to a full 31 pushes risk from 34% to 50%, but those 11 units are worth far more than the 16 points. I compared my own trips at matching prices and the full hold won every time, including the ones where I got stopped and paid. Spent weeks travelling light thinking I was being smart about it.

Capacity also goes past 26. Mine's at 31.

None of this really changes the guide, the shape of it was right. The one thing that actually changed how I play is the 25% bribe. Once you know that number, how much to carry mostly answers itself. If you've been running half loads like I was, try a few full ones.

Thanks again to Ignivar for the original, saved me a lot of guesswork.
