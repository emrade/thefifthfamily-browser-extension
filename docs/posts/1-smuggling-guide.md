200 smuggling runs logged: exact bribe costs, real risk numbers, and why I stopped travelling light

Ignivar's guide is what got me started and it's still what I go by. I've been logging every run since then, around 200 trades, 69 customs stops and 1,400+ risk readings, mostly just to work out my own routes. Some of it turned out to be worth sharing.

Most of the guide holds up exactly. Price band of 0.85x to 1.43x is spot on, buy prices all match, market shifts every 10 minutes like it says. Below is just where my numbers came out different, plus a couple of bits it doesn't cover.

Customs risk reads lower for me than the formula. Straight off the gauge: 0% full = 5%, 64% = 34%, 83% = 42%, 100% = 50%. Works out to roughly 5 + (fullness x 0.45). I've never seen it above 50%, even on a completely full hold. If anyone's seen higher, say so, because that would mean there's something going on I can't see from my own account.

Bribes are exactly 25% of cargo value. Every time. 69 stops, no variation at all. So 31 Diamonds is $155,000 of cargo and always a $38,750 bribe. You can work your exact cost out before you leave.

That 25% is behind the thing that surprised me most. Filling your hold is usually better than playing it safe, even though you get stopped more often. The bribe is a percentage rather than a flat fee, so every extra unit brings its own bribe along with it. Cost per unit is the same 25% whether you're carrying 5 or 31. So an extra unit still makes money as long as its margin beats 25% of the buy price, and most runs clear that easily. Going from 20 to a full 31 pushes risk from 34% to 50%, but those 11 units are worth far more than the 16 points. I compared my own trips at matching prices and the full hold won every time, including the ones where I got stopped and paid. Spent weeks travelling light thinking I was being smart about it.

Capacity also goes past 26. Mine's at 31.

Couple of small things in the price table that look like typos. Stolen Artwork's range should top out at 11,440, the Best Sell number there is already right. Forged Bonds' Best Sell also looks off, it's the same figure as Steroids, though I haven't run that one myself so I can't say for sure.

None of this really changes the guide, the shape of it was right. The one thing that actually changed how I play is the 25% bribe. Once you know that number, how much to carry mostly answers itself. If you've been running half loads to stay safe like I was, try a few full ones and see how they come out.

Thanks again to Ignivar for the original, saved me a lot of guesswork starting out.
