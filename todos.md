Halloween Massacre - 1st-31st October

Where can you earn Spirit?

    Crimes
    Careers
    Street Intels
    Fight Club (PvP)

Earning Halloween Spirit
The first success each server day at each of the 3 core mechanics (Crimes, Careers & Intels) offers an 100% success rate for your first spirit per mechanic. That's 3 guaranteed on your first success roll. Every roll after this is random @ 19.5% chance on success rolls only.

How many spirits can be dropped per day?
Across the 3 core mechanics:

    8 daily on weekdays.
    13 daily on weekends.
    18 for the final day (Oct 31st).

Achieving your maximum each day is enough to reap all of the rewards from the event - don't miss a day. Additionally if you do miss a day or if you want to compete to be right at the top of the leader board:

    PvP offers a permanent 1% chance drop rate. This is the only cap breaker.

How to use Spirits
The "Use Spirit" button appears underneath the "Execute Attack" button or directly on players profiles. Standard validity applies, you cannot use a spirit on someone already in hospital, jail, traveling or within the 10 minute hospital release grace period. Truces and beginner protection still apply. Using spirits does not provide respect, XP, cash, MG, affect the daily fight cap or use your stamina.

The attacker gets +1 Event Score and +10 Pumpkin Coins, plus +1 Family score if their Family is eligible. Using spirits breaks your own truce if you have used one.

Login Calendar
Logging in each calendar day unlocks a new Halloween reward across all 31 days. Rewards range from Halloween Family packs, pumpkin coins and resource packs (Energy, Stamina, Nerve).

Pumpkin Coin Shop
The Pumpkin Coin shop will list things such as Consumables, Family & Restore packs, Costumes & Reaper Set pieces.

Milestones
There are a range of Milestones to hit across the event. Daily login & core mechanic consistency will achieve this.

Family Competition
Every eligible Family runs its own separate leader board among its members, alongside the Global leader board.

Every Family with 10 or more members when the event starts on 1 October gets its own private leaderboard.

Scoring: every Halloween Spirit you land on a rival earns 1 point for your Family leader board. This is on top of the point it earns on the Global leader board. Spirit kills are the only way to score.

Who can score: you must be in an eligible Family when you land the hit. Kills made while you're Familyless, or in a Family that didn't qualify, don't count towards any Family board. They still count on the Global board.

Changing Family:

    If you leave your Family, your Family score resets to zero.
    If you're kicked, you keep your score.
    If your Don disbands the Family, everyone's Family score is wiped.
    If you join a new Family, your score goes with you and counts on their leader board.

Prizes: the top five places in every eligible Family win. Ties share the place and both get the full prize.

Family prizes are separate from Global prizes, so you can win both.
